# NILARIS go-to-market experiments

These are proposed experiments. The numbers are targets from the Project 3 presentation.

| Sprint | Goal | Target user or buyer | Channel | Key deliverable | Success metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Learn where repeated RFQ checks delay supplier enquiries. | Technical-office staff in industrial trading companies | LinkedIn and the founder’s professional network | A summary of recent cases, including which missing or ambiguous field caused rework | Conduct 10 interviews and document 6 recent problem examples. |
| Onboard | Test how quickly one company can turn its historical documents into schemas its experts can use. | A technical-office lead and designated reviewers at one company | Direct invitation to a seven-day trial | Three schemas for mutually agreed product types, with the company’s own expert review | Obtain 3 expert-approved schemas in 7 days at 1 company. |
| Commit | Test willingness to pay for a scoped pilot. | Industrial trading company budget holders | Direct pilot offers | A priced offer specifying scope, review responsibilities and expected pilot outcome | Deliver 3 scoped, priced offers and secure 1 paid pilot. |
